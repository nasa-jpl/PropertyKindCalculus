    Clocked time — a time referenced to a clock #

    A time read off a clock is a point on that clock's axis: an interval-scale quantity (timestamp), whose zero is the clock's own arbitrary origin, so two readings subtract into a duration (ratio scale) and nothing else combines them — in particular two readings do not add (the sum moves with the origin), and a reading of one clock is not a reading of another (the origins differ). The clock is a Frame: the identified reference the readings are relative to, as a coordinate frame is for components.

    ClockedTime clock R is deliberately its own structure, indexed by the clock, and not an InFrame alias: InFrame's generic add (licensed by a DifferenceKind) and toFrameScalar (scalars are frame-invariant, readings are not) are exactly the operations a clock reading must not have. No DifferenceKind timestamp is exposed. The licensed operations are sub (reading − reading = duration), shift (reading + duration = reading), and convert across clocks under an explicit ClockOffset.

    This is the kind of property a time model (MARS's Time instance of a network, SysML's @TimeModel) is an instance of, at any carrier R — Nat ticks, Rat, Real seconds.
