    instance PropertyKindCalculus.instScalarCarrierReal :
    ScalarCarrier ℝ

    ℝ is a scalar carrier: a real magnitude is one number, and ℝ's * is the multiplication of magnitudes. This is what makes Quantity.mul available at the proof representation — and, by its absence at Fin n → ℝ, what keeps the componentwise product of two vector quantities from being signed as a product of kinds.
