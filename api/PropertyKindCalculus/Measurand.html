    structure PropertyKindCalculus.Measurand (k k₂ : KindOfProperty) (S R : Type) :
    Type

    A measurand (VIM 2.3), at its kind. A quantity of kind k a model exposes to be measured on a system/state S, carried at R: best estimate, variance at the squared kind k₂ (licensed by the authored edge square), and the indication predicate. The kind indices are the data the prose tradition has nowhere to put.

    • square : ProductKind k k k₂

      The authored square edge — a measurand exists only where the model's kind algebra has sanctioned k · k.

    • estimate : S → Quantity k R

      GUM's best estimate, in a state, at the measurand's kind.

    • variance : S → Quantity k₂ R

      The second moment about the estimate, at the squared kind.

    • IsIndication : Quantity k R → Prop

      The values a measurement can return, carrying the measurand's kind.
