            def PropertyKindCalculus.Uncertainty.combinedQ {R : Type} [Paradigm.NumCarrier R] {kO : KindOfProperty} (contribs : List (Quantity kO R)) :
            Quantity kO R

            Combined standard uncertainty u_c(y) = √(Σ uᵢ(y)²) — the quadrature of the (output-kind, homogeneous) contributions, so itself a Quantity kₒ R. Summing in quadrature is the independence assumption of GUM (§4.4). Computes on the carrier R and re-stamps kₒ; run at R := Adequacy it adequacy-checks its own sum of squares.
