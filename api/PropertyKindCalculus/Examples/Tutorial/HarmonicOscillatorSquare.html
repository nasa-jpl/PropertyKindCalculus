                                                        Stiffness: the same parts, two sorts of whole — what the Kinds corner buys #

                                                        Two springs in parallel: k = kA + kB. The same two springs in series: 1/k = 1/kA + 1/kB. One attribute, two sorts of whole, two different aggregation facts — the oscillator's own instance of volume aggregates over a rigid assembly and contracts over a mixture. A registry keyed by the kind alone must license both or neither.
